# Code of Conduct

OpenMicBoard aims to be a respectful, constructive community. Participate in good faith; welcome people with different experience levels; focus criticism on ideas and changes; and respect privacy and consent.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, or sharing private information without permission are not acceptable.

If you experience or witness a violation, report it privately to the project maintainers. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the community.
